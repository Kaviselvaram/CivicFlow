# 02 — User Personas

## Persona 1: Maya Chen — The Active Citizen
- **Age:** 34
- **Occupation:** Architectural Designer & Neighborhood Association Member
- **Location:** Oakwood Ward 4
- **Tech Savviness:** High (Uses mobile devices, appreciates intuitive micro-interactions and editorial clarity)
- **Goals:** Wants hazardous potholes and broken playground lighting near her children's school repaired swiftly without filling out a 4-page PDF or calling an unmanned hotline.
- **Frustrations:** "I reported a sidewalk sinkhole two months ago on the old city portal. It said 'Ticket 8941 closed,' but the hole is still there, now full of stagnant water."
- **Key Needs:**
  - One-handed mobile reporting while walking her dog.
  - Transparent progress alerts when a crew is actually assigned.
  - Ability to inspect the after-repair photo before marking it closed.

## Persona 2: David O'Connor — Field Operations Supervisor
- **Age:** 48
- **Occupation:** Operations Superintendent, Department of Transportation & Public Works
- **Location:** Municipal Operations Center
- **Tech Savviness:** Moderate (Values data density, rapid filtering, keyboard shortcuts, clear priority cues)
- **Goals:** Triage incoming reports every morning, dispatch asphalt patching trucks along optimal spatial clusters, prevent citizen escalations.
- **Frustrations:** "We get 30 calls describing 'the big hole near the bakery' with no GPS, or low-priority paint scuffs marked as emergency hazards while real water main leaks get buried."
- **Key Needs:**
  - Map-based spatial grouping to prevent sending three different trucks to the same street.
  - Automated AI triage that suggests department routing and priority based on keywords and photo.
  - Ability to upload resolution photos right from the dispatch tablet so citizens see the completed asphalt patch.

## Persona 3: Marcus Vance — Municipal Director of Public Services
- **Age:** 55
- **Occupation:** City Manager / Deputy Mayor of Infrastructure
- **Tech Savviness:** Moderate (Needs executive rollups, SLA adherence metrics, citizen satisfaction tracking)
- **Goals:** Prove civic accountability to the city council, monitor departmental resolution velocity, allocate annual infrastructure budget accurately.
- **Key Needs:**
  - Resolution verification rates by ward.
  - Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR) trends across departments.
  - Public transparency dashboard showing active vs resolved issues.
